Fig. X (provisional caption, E2). Study area. The 142 transport analysis zones (UTAM) of the 2023 mobility survey frame, grouped into urban Bogota (111 UTAM), rural Bogota (5 UPR units) and the ring of 26 neighbouring municipalities (hatched). For 22 of the 26 units in that ring no usable DANE census population is available, so population-based indicators are reported for Bogota only. Dots mark existing public EV charging stations as mapped in OpenStreetMap (Overpass export, OSM base 2026-09-01; n = 42). OSM is a collaborative source and may undercount private or recently opened stations; this layer is provisional and will be replaced if an official registry is adopted. The star marks the historic city centre; labels name major localities (Bogota) and municipalities (region).
